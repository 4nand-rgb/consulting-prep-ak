# MECE Issue Tree

## Main Problem

Why are NHS waiting times increasing?

---

# 1. Capacity Constraints

## Workforce Shortages
- Doctor shortages
- Nurse shortages
- Burnout and retention issues

## Infrastructure Limitations
- Limited hospital beds
- Limited theatre capacity
- Diagnostic bottlenecks

---

# 2. Rising Demand

## Demographic Changes
- Ageing population
- Chronic disease growth

## Increased Patient Demand
- Backlog from previous delays
- Higher healthcare utilisation

---

# 3. Operational Inefficiencies

## Scheduling Problems
- Delayed appointments
- Inefficient theatre scheduling

## Referral Bottlenecks
- Slow referral pathways
- Poor patient flow coordination

---

# 4. Technology and Data Gaps

## Fragmented Systems
- Non-integrated EHR systems
- Data silos across trusts

## Limited Digital Adoption
- Manual administrative processes
- Poor operational visibility

---

# Strategic Implication

The NHS waiting list issue is not caused by one single factor.

It is a combination of:
- operational constraints
- workforce pressures
- rising demand
- technology limitations

This means solutions must address multiple areas simultaneously rather than relying on a single intervention.
