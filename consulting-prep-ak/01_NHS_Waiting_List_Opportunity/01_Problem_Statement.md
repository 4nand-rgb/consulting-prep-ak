# Problem Statement

The NHS waiting list challenge is not only a healthcare access issue. It is also an operational, financial, and strategic problem.

The key question is:

**How can the NHS reduce waiting times while managing workforce, funding, and capacity constraints?**

This project explores whether independent sector partnerships, digital triage, operational improvement, and better resource allocation can help reduce pressure on NHS services.

## Why This Matters

- NHS demand continues to rise
- Workforce shortages remain significant
- Patients face long waiting times
- Healthcare systems require operational efficiency
- Private providers and digital health firms may support recovery

## Objective

To analyse the strategic and operational opportunities created by NHS waiting lists using consulting-style frameworks and structured business analysis.
