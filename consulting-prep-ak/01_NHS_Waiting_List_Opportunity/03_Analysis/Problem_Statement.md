# NHS Waiting List Problem Statement

## Situation

NHS waiting lists have increased significantly due to rising demand, operational pressure, workforce shortages, and diagnostic bottlenecks.

---

## Core Problem

Demand for healthcare services is increasing faster than operational capacity.

This creates:
- treatment delays
- patient dissatisfaction
- workforce pressure
- operational inefficiencies

---

## Key Drivers

1. Workforce shortages
2. Diagnostic delays
3. Bed capacity limitations
4. Referral growth
5. Operational inefficiencies

---

## Strategic Importance

Reducing waiting times is critical for:
- patient outcomes
- NHS productivity
- financial sustainability
- political priorities

---

## Initial Hypothesis

Operational inefficiencies and capacity constraints are major contributors to increasing waiting times.

---

## Potential Focus Areas

- scheduling optimisation
- diagnostic expansion
- digital health tools
- patient flow redesign
- private sector partnerships

---

## Consulting Goal

Develop practical recommendations supported by operational and strategic analysis.
