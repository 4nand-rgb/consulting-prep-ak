# LinkedIn Post Draft

NHS waiting lists are usually discussed as a healthcare access problem.

But from a consulting perspective, they are also an operational and strategic problem.

The issue can be broken into four main drivers:

1. Capacity constraints  
2. Rising healthcare demand  
3. Operational inefficiencies  
4. Technology and data gaps  

This creates opportunities for:
- better capacity planning
- improved patient flow
- private sector partnerships
- digital health solutions
- stronger workforce planning

The key lesson for healthcare consulting is simple:

Complex healthcare problems rarely have one solution.

They require structured thinking, operational analysis, and practical implementation.

This is the focus of my first GitHub portfolio project: NHS Waiting List Opportunity.
