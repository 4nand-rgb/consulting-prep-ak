# Business Model Framework

A business model explains how an organisation creates and delivers value.

## Core Elements

1. Customer Segments
2. Value Proposition
3. Revenue Streams
4. Cost Structure
5. Key Activities
6. Key Resources

## Healthcare Example

### Community Pharmacy

Customers:
- patients
- NHS

Revenue:
- dispensing income
- private services

Costs:
- staff
- rent
- stock

## Consulting Use

Used in:
- profitability analysis
- market strategy
- healthcare consulting

## Learning Note

Business models explain how organisations operate and make money.
