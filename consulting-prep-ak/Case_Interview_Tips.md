# Case Interview Tips

## Core Principles

1. Structure first
2. Think logically
3. Communicate clearly
4. Prioritise key drivers
5. Stay hypothesis-driven

---

## Strong Candidate Behaviour

- organised thinking
- confidence
- business judgement
- clear communication
- calm under pressure

---

## Common Mistakes

- jumping into analysis
- weak structure
- random brainstorming
- poor communication

---

## Healthcare Consulting Tip

Use patient, operational, and financial perspectives together.

---

## Learning Note

Case interviews reward structured thinking more than perfect answers.
