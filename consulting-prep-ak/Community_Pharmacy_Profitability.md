# Community Pharmacy Profitability Project

## Objective

Analyse profitability challenges facing UK community pharmacies.

---

## Key Areas

1. Prescription margins
2. Staffing costs
3. Inventory costs
4. NHS reimbursement
5. Private service opportunities

---

## Strategic Questions

- Why are margins shrinking?
- Which services improve profitability?
- How can operations improve?
