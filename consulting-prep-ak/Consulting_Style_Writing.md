# Consulting Style Writing

Consulting writing should be:
- clear
- concise
- structured
- evidence-based

---

## Good Writing

- short sentences
- logical flow
- direct recommendations

---

## Avoid

- unnecessary jargon
- vague statements
- long paragraphs

---

## Healthcare Example

Weak:
The NHS has many complicated issues.

Strong:
NHS waiting times are increasing because demand exceeds capacity.

---

## Learning Note

Consulting writing prioritises clarity and decision-making.
