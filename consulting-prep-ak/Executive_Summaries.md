# Executive Summaries

Executive summaries provide the most important information quickly.

## Main Components

1. Problem
2. Key Findings
3. Recommendation
4. Expected Impact

---

## Healthcare Example

Problem:
NHS waiting lists are increasing.

Recommendation:
Improve operational efficiency and expand diagnostics.

Impact:
Reduced delays and better patient access.

---

## Consulting Use

Used in:
- board reports
- strategy documents
- consulting presentations

---

## Learning Note

Executive summaries should be concise, structured, and action-focused.
