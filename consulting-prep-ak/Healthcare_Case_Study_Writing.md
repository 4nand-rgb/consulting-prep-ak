# Healthcare Case Study Writing

Healthcare case studies analyse healthcare problems using business frameworks.

## Structure

1. Background
2. Problem
3. Analysis
4. Findings
5. Recommendation

---

## Example Topics

- NHS waiting lists
- pharmacy profitability
- digital transformation

---

## Consulting Focus

A strong case study includes:
- operational analysis
- financial impact
- stakeholder considerations

---

## Learning Note

Healthcare case studies combine healthcare and strategy thinking.
