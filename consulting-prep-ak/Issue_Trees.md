# Issue Trees

Issue trees break large business problems into smaller logical parts.

## Why They Matter

They help:
- structure analysis
- identify root causes
- organise thinking

## Healthcare Example

### Why are NHS waiting times increasing?

1. Capacity constraints
2. Rising demand
3. Operational inefficiencies
4. Technology limitations

## Good Principles

Issue trees should be:
- MECE
- logical
- complete

## Consulting Use

Used in:
- case interviews
- operational analysis
- strategy projects

## Learning Note

Issue trees convert complex problems into clear structures.
