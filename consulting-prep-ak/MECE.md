# MECE Framework

MECE stands for:

- Mutually Exclusive
- Collectively Exhaustive

It is one of the most important problem-structuring frameworks used in consulting.

The purpose of MECE is to break complex business problems into clear, non-overlapping categories.

---

# Why MECE Matters

Consultants use MECE to:
- structure business problems
- improve communication
- avoid overlapping ideas
- ensure complete analysis

MECE improves:
- case interview performance
- strategic thinking
- executive communication
- consulting-style writing

---

# Simple Example

## Problem:
Why are NHS waiting times increasing?

## MECE Structure:

1. Capacity Constraints
2. Rising Demand
3. Operational Inefficiencies
4. Technology Limitations

Each category is:
- separate
- non-overlapping
- collectively complete

---

# Non-MECE Example

- Doctors
- Hospitals
- NHS
- Patients

This is NOT MECE because categories overlap and are incomplete.

---

# Consulting Application

MECE is commonly used in:
- case interviews
- issue trees
- market analysis
- strategic recommendations
- executive summaries
