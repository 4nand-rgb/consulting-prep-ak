# Market Entry Cases

Market entry cases evaluate whether a company should enter a new market.

## Core Questions

1. Is the market attractive?
2. Can the company compete?
3. Is entry profitable?
4. What risks exist?

---

## Healthcare Example

### Digital Health Startup Entering NHS

Questions:
- NHS demand?
- competition?
- procurement barriers?
- regulation?
- integration capability?

---

## Common Risks

- regulation
- strong competitors
- operational complexity
- high investment costs

---

## Learning Note

Market entry requires balancing opportunity with feasibility.
