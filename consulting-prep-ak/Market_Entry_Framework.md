# Market Entry Framework

Market entry analysis evaluates whether a company should enter a market.

## Key Questions

1. Is the market attractive?
2. Can the company compete?
3. Is it financially viable?

## Entry Options

- partnership
- acquisition
- internal build
- pilot launch

## Healthcare Example

### Digital Health Startup

Questions:
- NHS demand?
- regulatory barriers?
- integration capability?

Used in:

## Learning Note

Market entry evaluates risks and opportunities realistically.
