# Market Sizing

Market sizing estimates the size of a market using assumptions and logic.

## Why It Matters

Consultants use market sizing to:
- estimate opportunities
- support strategy
- assess feasibility

---

## Common Structure

Market Size =
Number of Customers × Average Spending

---

## Healthcare Example

### Estimate UK Community Pharmacy Flu Vaccine Market

Assumptions:
- UK population
- eligible adults
- vaccination rate
- average vaccine revenue

---

## Consulting Skills Tested

- logical thinking
- assumptions
- communication
- numerical reasoning

---

## Learning Note

Market sizing rewards structured thinking more than exact numbers.
