# Market Sizing Framework

Market sizing is one of the most common consulting interview and strategy analysis skills.

The purpose is to estimate:
- market opportunity
- revenue potential
- customer demand
- industry size

Consultants use market sizing when exact data is unavailable.

---

# Why Market Sizing Matters

Market sizing helps organisations:
- evaluate business opportunities
- assess growth potential
- support investment decisions
- prioritise strategic markets

It is commonly used in:
- consulting interviews
- healthcare strategy
- digital health analysis
- market entry projects
- business planning

---

# Core Market Sizing Structure

## Total Market

How large is the overall market opportunity?

↓

## Target Segment

Which customer or patient group is relevant?

↓

## Revenue Opportunity

What portion of the market can realistically be captured?

---

# Top-Down Approach

Start with the overall market size and narrow down.

Example:

UK population
↓
Patients requiring elective care
↓
Patients on NHS waiting lists
↓
Private sector treatment opportunity

---

# Bottom-Up Approach

Start with smaller operational assumptions and build upward.

Example:

Patients treated per day
×
Average treatment value
×
Number of hospitals
=
Estimated market size

---

# Healthcare Example

## Problem

What is the private healthcare opportunity created by NHS waiting lists?

---

## Possible Assumptions

- Number of patients waiting
- Percentage seeking private care
- Average treatment value
- Capacity of private providers

---

## Strategic Questions

Consultants may ask:

- How large is the opportunity?
- Is demand growing?
- Which services generate the highest value?
- Which patient groups are most attractive?
- Which regions have highest unmet demand?

---

# Common Mistakes

## 1. Unrealistic Assumptions

Assumptions should remain logical and explainable.

---

## 2. Missing Business Context

Market size alone is not enough.

Consultants also consider:
- competition
- regulation
- operational feasibility
- workforce availability
