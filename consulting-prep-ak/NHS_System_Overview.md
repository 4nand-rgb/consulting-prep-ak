# NHS System Overview

The NHS is the publicly funded healthcare system in the UK.

## Core Areas

1. Primary Care
2. Secondary Care
3. Community Care
4. Mental Health Services
5. Integrated Care Systems

---

## Primary Care

Includes:
- GP practices
- community pharmacies
- dentists

---

## Secondary Care

Includes:
- hospitals
- specialist treatment
- emergency care

---

- ageing population
