# NHS Waiting List Opportunity Project

## Objective

Analyse NHS waiting list pressures and identify operational improvement opportunities.

---

## Core Questions

1. What is driving waiting list growth?
2. Which specialties are most affected?
3. Where are operational bottlenecks?
4. Which interventions could improve capacity?

---

## Analysis Areas

- referral growth
- workforce shortages
- bed capacity
- diagnostics
- patient flow
- regional variation

---

## Potential Solutions

- operational redesign
- digital scheduling
- private sector partnerships
- diagnostic expansion
- AI forecasting
