# Porter’s Five Forces

Porter’s Five Forces is one of the most important business strategy frameworks.

It was developed by Michael Porter and is widely used in:
- consulting
- MBA strategy
- healthcare analysis
- competitive strategy
- market assessment

The framework helps organisations understand industry competitiveness and profitability.

---

# The Five Forces

1. Competitive Rivalry
2. Threat of New Entrants
3. Bargaining Power of Buyers
4. Bargaining Power of Suppliers
5. Threat of Substitutes

---

# 1. Competitive Rivalry

This examines how intensely companies compete within an industry.

High rivalry may reduce:
- profitability
- pricing power
- market share stability

Example:
Competition among EHR vendors in UK healthcare technology.

---

# 2. Threat of New Entrants

This evaluates how easily new competitors can enter the market.

Barriers may include:
- regulation
- technology requirements
- capital investment
- brand reputation

Healthcare markets often have high barriers due to regulation and operational complexity.

---

# 3. Bargaining Power of Buyers

Buyers have high power when they can strongly influence pricing or demand.

In healthcare:
- NHS organisations
- healthcare commissioners
- large hospital systems

may have strong negotiating power.

---

# 4. Bargaining Power of Suppliers

Suppliers have high power when few alternatives exist.

Examples:
- specialised software vendors
- pharmaceutical suppliers
- medical device companies

---

# 5. Threat of Substitutes

Substitutes are alternative solutions that may replace existing services.

Examples:
- telemedicine replacing some physical visits
- digital pharmacies
- AI-enabled triage systems

---

# Healthcare Example

## Industry:
UK NHS Digital Health Market

### Competitive Rivalry
High competition among EHR and digital health firms.

### New Entrants
Moderate barriers due to regulation and NHS procurement complexity.

### Buyer Power
NHS organisations hold significant purchasing power.

### Supplier Power
Technology vendors may hold specialised expertise.

### Substitutes
Alternative digital platforms and AI solutions may disrupt existing systems.
