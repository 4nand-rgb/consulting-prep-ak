# Profitability Cases

Profitability cases analyse why profits are increasing or decreasing.

## Core Formula

Profit = Revenue - Cost

---

## Revenue Drivers

1. Price
2. Volume
3. Product Mix

---

## Cost Drivers

### Fixed Costs
- rent
- salaries
- equipment

### Variable Costs
- materials
- logistics
- labour

---

## Healthcare Example

### Community Pharmacy

Possible profit problems:
- reduced prescription margins
- rising staffing costs
- lower customer volume

---

## Consulting Questions

- Has revenue changed?
- Have costs increased?
- Which segment is underperforming?
- Is the issue internal or external?

---

## Learning Note

Profitability frameworks simplify complex business problems into revenue and cost drivers.
