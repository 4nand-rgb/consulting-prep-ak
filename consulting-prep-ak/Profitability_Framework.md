# Profitability Framework

The profitability framework is one of the most important consulting problem-solving frameworks.

It is commonly used in:
- case interviews
- strategy consulting
- operational improvement
- healthcare business analysis

The purpose is to identify why a company or organisation is experiencing profit growth or decline.

---

# Core Formula

Profit = Revenue - Costs

Consultants break profitability problems into two major areas:

1. Revenue
2. Costs

---

# Revenue Analysis

Revenue is affected by:

Revenue = Price × Volume

---

## Price Factors

- pricing strategy
- reimbursement rates
- service pricing
- discounts
- competitive pricing

---

## Volume Factors

- number of customers
- patient demand
- prescription volume
- market share
- service utilisation

---

# Cost Analysis

Costs are usually divided into:

## Fixed Costs

Costs that remain relatively stable.

Examples:
- rent
- infrastructure
- salaried staff
- technology systems

---

## Variable Costs

Costs that increase with activity volume.

Examples:
- medical supplies
- staffing overtime
- logistics
- utilities

---

# Healthcare Example

## Problem

Why are UK community pharmacies becoming less profitable?

---

## Revenue Issues

- reduced reimbursement margins
- lower dispensing profitability
- competitive pricing pressure

---

## Cost Issues

- rising labour costs
- inflation
- operational expenses
- supply chain pressures

---

# Strategic Questions

Consultants may ask:

- Is revenue declining?
- Are costs increasing?
- Which services are most profitable?
- Which operational areas are inefficient?
- Can pricing or operational models improve?

---

# Consulting Application

The profitability framework is useful for:
- healthcare strategy
- pharmacy analysis
- operational improvement
- NHS productivity analysis
- business turnaround projects
