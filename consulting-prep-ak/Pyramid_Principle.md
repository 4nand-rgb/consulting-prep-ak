# Pyramid Principle

The Pyramid Principle is one of the most important consulting communication frameworks.

It was developed by Barbara Minto, a former McKinsey consultant.

The framework helps consultants structure ideas clearly and communicate recommendations efficiently.

---

# Core Idea

Start with the main recommendation first.

Then support it with logical arguments and evidence.

Structure:

Main Recommendation
↓
Supporting Arguments
↓
Evidence and Analysis

---

# Why It Matters

Executives and clients often have limited time.

The Pyramid Principle helps:
- improve clarity
- reduce unnecessary detail
- communicate recommendations quickly
- structure consulting reports effectively

---

# Simple Example

## Recommendation

The NHS should expand operational efficiency programs and digital transformation initiatives.

---

## Supporting Arguments

1. Workforce shortages reduce operational capacity
2. Scheduling inefficiencies increase delays
3. Fragmented systems reduce coordination

---

## Evidence

- Rising waiting lists
- Delayed referrals
- Diagnostic bottlenecks
- Administrative inefficiencies

---

# Difference Between Student Writing and Consulting Writing

## Traditional Student Writing

- Background
- More background
- Discussion
- Conclusion at the end

---

## Consulting Writing

- Recommendation first
- Supporting arguments second
- Evidence third

Consultants prioritise:
- clarity
- structure
- business implications

---

# Healthcare Example

## Main Recommendation

The NHS should improve patient flow management.

## Supporting Points

1. Better scheduling may reduce delays
2. Digital systems may improve coordination
3. Operational redesign may improve resource utilisation

---

# Consulting Application

The Pyramid Principle is commonly used in:
- consulting presentations
- executive summaries
- strategy reports
- healthcare recommendations
- business articles
