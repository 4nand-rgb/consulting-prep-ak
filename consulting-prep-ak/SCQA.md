# SCQA Framework

SCQA stands for:

- Situation
- Complication
- Question
- Answer

It is a structured storytelling framework widely used in consulting, executive communication, and strategy presentations.

---

# Why SCQA Matters

SCQA helps consultants:
- explain problems clearly
- create logical flow
- structure presentations
- improve executive communication
- write consulting-style reports

It is commonly used in:
- McKinsey-style presentations
- executive summaries
- consulting reports
- business articles
- strategic recommendations

---

# Structure of SCQA

## 1. Situation

Describe the current situation or context.

Example:
The NHS continues to face increasing patient demand and operational pressure.

---

## 2. Complication

Explain the problem or challenge.

Example:
Waiting times remain high due to workforce shortages and limited capacity.

---

## 3. Question

Identify the key business question.

Example:
How can the NHS reduce waiting times while maintaining operational efficiency?

---

## 4. Answer

Provide the recommendation or proposed solution.

Example:
The NHS should improve operational efficiency, expand digital transformation, and strengthen private sector partnerships.

---

# Healthcare Example

## Situation
The NHS faces rising healthcare demand.

## Complication
Operational capacity remains limited.

## Question
How can patient access improve without significantly increasing costs?

## Answer
Operational redesign and digital transformation may improve efficiency and patient flow.
