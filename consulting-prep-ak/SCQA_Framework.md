# SCQA Framework

SCQA is a consulting communication structure.

S = Situation  
C = Complication  
Q = Question  
A = Answer

---

## 1. Situation

Provide background context.

Example:
The NHS is facing rising patient demand.

---

## 2. Complication

Explain the problem or tension.

Example:
Waiting lists continue increasing despite additional funding.

---

## 3. Question

Define the key business question.

Example:
How can the NHS reduce waiting times efficiently?

---

## 4. Answer

Provide recommendation or solution.

Example:
Improve operational efficiency and expand diagnostic capacity.

---

## Consulting Use

Used in:
- consulting reports
- executive summaries
- presentations
- recommendations

---

## Learning Note

SCQA creates clear and logical business communication.
