# SWOT Analysis

SWOT stands for:

- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Opportunities
- Threats

## Strengths
Internal advantages.

## Weaknesses
Internal limitations.

## Opportunities
External growth possibilities.

## Threats
External risks.

## Healthcare Example

### NHS Waiting Lists

Strengths:
- trusted system
- skilled workforce

Weaknesses:
- staff shortages
- delays

Opportunities:
- digital health
- private partnerships

Threats:
- rising demand
- funding pressure

## Consulting Use

Used for:
- strategic planning
- healthcare analysis
- business assessment

## Learning Note

SWOT organises internal and external factors clearly.
