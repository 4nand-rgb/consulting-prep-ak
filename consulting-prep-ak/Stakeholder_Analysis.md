# Stakeholder Analysis

Stakeholder analysis identifies people affected by decisions or projects.

## Key Questions

1. Who is affected?
2. What do they care about?
3. How much influence do they have?

## Healthcare Example

### NHS Digital Transformation

Stakeholders:
- patients
- clinicians
- NHS managers
- regulators
- suppliers

## Consulting Use

Used in:
- implementation
- healthcare projects
- change management

## Learning Note

Successful strategy depends on stakeholder alignment.
